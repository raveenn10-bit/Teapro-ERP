# TeaPro ERP Brand Guide

The leaf represents Sri Lankan tea and agriculture; the rising data nodes represent measurement, analytics and controlled business growth. The mark is original to TeaPro ERP.

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Forest Green | `#14532D` | primary actions and mark |
| Leaf Green | `#86BC49` | accent and positive signals |
| Dark Green | `#14251D` | navigation and premium backgrounds |
| Ivory | `#F5F7F2` | surfaces and print-safe background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | contrast |

Keep clear space around the mark equal to one quarter of the icon width. Do not stretch, rotate, recolor with low contrast, add shadows or place the full-color mark on busy imagery without a solid surface.

Use `teapro-thermal.svg` for one-color receipts. Businesses may upload their own logo for customer-facing bills; TeaPro application/installer identity remains unchanged.

The SVG files are the masters. PNG and ICO outputs are generated from these sources and should be regenerated after any master change.
